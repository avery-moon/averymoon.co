About

I build practical systems that make complex work easier to navigate.

I help organizations work smarter by improving the systems people rely on every day.

Throughout my career, I've been drawn to designing processes, documenting knowledge, improving workflows, and building business systems that help people learn faster, make better decisions, and work more effectively.

My background spans aerospace manufacturing, Amazon Robotics, education partnerships, and community programs, but the common thread has never been the industry. It has always been creating clarity from complexity. I'm most energized by building systems that continue creating value long after implementation.

My best work happens at the intersection of knowledge management, process design, and organizational effectiveness. I enjoy helping organizations become more effective, resilient, and accessible by improving how people, information, and processes work together.

At the core of my work is a longstanding interest in accessibility, not just in the traditional sense, but in how people gain access to knowledge, opportunities, resources, and communities. I've consistently been drawn to making complex systems easier to understand, navigate, and improve.

Better systems. Better outcomes. Stronger teams.
